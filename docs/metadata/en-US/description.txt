Anatti turns your app's screens into store-ready screenshots and app preview videos, sized correctly for the App Store and Google Play. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build screenshots at every size the App Store and Google Play require
• Create app preview videos that follow store rules for length, format and resolution
• Add captions and backgrounds, and organize your work in projects
• Use the app in 7 languages: English, Spanish, German, Finnish, Swedish, French and Italian

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Anatti works entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics. The screenshots you choose never leave your phone.

ANATTI PRO
Exporting requires Anatti Pro, a monthly auto-renewing subscription.

SUBSCRIPTION DETAILS
• Anatti Pro costs 7.99 EUR per month (price may vary by country).
• Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
• The subscription renews automatically every month unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings after purchase.
